    YouTube logging

    A watch only reaches your YouTube history — and your recommendations — while the embedded player still recognises you as signed in. It can lapse silently, so this app reads your history back and reports any video you watched here that never arrived.

    Jev curation rules

    What you're looking for

    Background that Jev reads alongside every video. Describe the viewer and the kind of material worth keeping — it colours all of the questions below.

    Must be true

    Every one of these has to pass before a video is allowed through. Ask for one thing at a time — Jev answers exactly what you write, so keep each question literal.

    Red flags

    If any of these turns out to be true, the video is rejected no matter what else passed.

    Optional rating

    Ask Jev to place each video on a scale you define. Jev's numbers are compressed — even strong videos often land in the upper-middle — so test a few videos before trusting the minimum.

    Transcripts

    Jev reads a transcript when one exists, which is what rescues good videos with clickbait titles. Very short transcripts say less than the title does, so they are ignored rather than fed in.
